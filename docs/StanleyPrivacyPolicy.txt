PRIVACY POLICY

Stanley, a Discord bot for Skullgirls Mobile fighter information

Last updated: 19 October 2026
Effective: 19 October 2026


1. WHO THIS POLICY IS FROM

Stanley ("Stanley", the "Bot") is a Discord bot operated by The SGM Wiki
Team ("we", "us"). This policy explains what personal information Stanley
handles, why, how long it is kept, and how to have it deleted. It covers the
Bot only, not Discord itself and not any website the Bot links to.

Contact for any privacy question or request: dompark3147@gmail.com


2. THE SHORT VERSION

  - If you only read Stanley's replies, we store nothing about you.
  - Stanley works only through slash commands. It cannot read your
    messages: it uses none of Discord's privileged intents (Message
    Content, Server Members, Presence).
  - The only personal information Stanley keeps is the Discord user ID of
    an editor against each change they make to fighter or alias data.
  - We do not sell, rent or trade personal information, we show no
    advertising, and we run no tracking or profiling.


3. WHAT STANLEY HANDLES, AND WHY

a) Reading commands (/fighter, /wiki, /alias list, /credits)

When you run a command, Discord sends Stanley the information it needs to
reply: your Discord user ID, the server and channel the command came from,
and the text you typed as the command's options. Stanley uses this in memory
to build the reply and then discards it. None of it is written to the
database.

Why: to answer the command you ran.

b) Editing commands (/fighter-admin, /alias-admin)

When an editor adds, edits or removes fighter or alias data, Stanley writes
a change record containing the editor's Discord user ID, the time of the
change, the action, and the values before and after it. This is the only
place Stanley stores a Discord user ID.

Why: so that incorrect or abusive edits to shared community data can be
reviewed, attributed and undone. This is a legitimate interest in the
integrity of the data; for editors in the UK/EU, that is our lawful basis.

c) Fighter data itself

Stanley's database holds Skullgirls Mobile game information: characters,
fighters (name, character, rarity, element, ability name, base stats and a
portrait URL), signature ability text, and aliases. Portraits are not
hosted by us; only their external URLs are stored. This is game
information, not personal information about you.

d) Operational logs

The server keeps technical logs so faults can be diagnosed. A log entry
about an error may include the name of the command, the server ID and the
Discord user ID of the person who ran it. Logs stay on the server in a
rotating buffer that the Bot's container configuration caps at three files
of 10 MB per container: once the cap is reached, the oldest entries are
deleted automatically. They are not copied anywhere else, are not part of
the database backup, and are not used to build any profile.

Why: keeping the Bot working and secure.


4. WHAT STANLEY NEVER COLLECTS

  - the content of your messages, in servers or in DMs
  - member lists, roles, presence or online status, voice activity
  - your email address, real name, address or any payment information
  - attachments, images or files you post
  - information about people who only read Stanley's replies
  - anything through cookies, trackers or third-party analytics


5. WHO ELSE IS INVOLVED

We share personal information with no one for their own purposes. It is
handled on our behalf by:

  - Discord Inc., the platform Stanley runs on. Everything you send to
    Stanley passes through Discord first and is also covered by Discord's
    own privacy policy.
  - netcup GmbH, which hosts the single server running the Bot and its
    PostgreSQL database, in a United States or European Union region.
  - the off-site storage holding the nightly database backup.

We will disclose information if we are legally required to, for example in
response to a valid legal process, and we may disclose it where necessary
to investigate abuse of the Bot.

Separately, your Discord client loads fighter portraits from external image
URLs and you may follow links to the Skullgirls Mobile Fandom wiki. Those
requests go to third parties under their own privacy policies; we receive
nothing from them.


6. WHERE DATA IS HELD, AND FOR HOW LONG

The database runs on one server in a United States or European Union
region. If you are in the UK or EU, your information may therefore 
be transferred outside your country; we rely on appropriate safeguards 
for any such transfer.

Retention:

  - reading-command data: not retained (discarded once the reply is sent)
  - change records: kept while Stanley operates, so edits stay auditable,
    or until a deletion request is completed (section 7)
  - operational logs: no fixed period. They are capped by size, and the
    oldest entries are deleted automatically as new ones arrive, which in
    normal operation is a window of days rather than months
  - backups: each nightly backup is kept only until newer ones replace it.
    A deletion is applied to the live database as soon as it is completed,
    and can still appear in a stored backup copy until that copy is
    replaced


7. YOUR RIGHTS AND HOW TO USE THEM

You may ask us to:

  - tell you what information we hold about you, and give you a copy;
  - correct information that is wrong;
  - delete your Discord user ID from Stanley's change records. We complete
    such requests within 30 days. The record of the change itself is kept
    for data integrity, with the editor's ID replaced by an anonymous
    marker;
  - object to or restrict our handling of your information.

Email nhatnm.work@gmail.com from an address we can act on, or ask us in
Stanley's support channel on Discord, and tell us your Discord username and
user ID. We may ask you to confirm, through Discord, that the account is
yours.

Depending on where you live you may also have the right to complain to a
data protection authority: in New Zealand, the Office of the Privacy
Commissioner; in the EU or UK, your national supervisory authority.


8. CHILDREN

Stanley is not directed at children under Discord's minimum age for their
country, and we do not knowingly keep information about them. If you
believe we hold such information, email us and we will delete it.


9. SECURITY

The database is not reachable from the internet; the bot token and the
database password are held outside the source repository; access to the
server is limited to the maintainers; backups are kept off the server. No
system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but
the information Stanley holds is deliberately kept to a minimum.


10. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy. The current version is always the one published
in the project's public GitHub repository, at the URL entered in the Discord
Developer Portal and linked from /credits, with the date of the last change
at the top. Material changes are announced in Stanley's support channel on
Discord where practical. Continuing to use Stanley after a change means you
accept the updated policy.
